Encumbered with his empty hands, he hurries away.

Because it's raining. Very strong.

He goes to take shelter in a café.
In the background, a screen is showing a match.
Penalty.

Number 9 sets off.

He misses the target.
He stares at the screen for a second too long.

Something is loose.

His legs no longer respond.

He collapses between two tables.
We shake it. He opens one eye.

He sees the decrepit ceiling of the cafe. A crack.
No sentence to make up for it.

No analysis.

Only distant voices.
He stammers:
— Dad…

I saw it all...

and I didn't hold anything...
He cries.

Without noise.


A burly guy approaches.
He slaps him hard on the back—a little too hard, as always.
- Hey. Grab that beer. It will perk you up!
