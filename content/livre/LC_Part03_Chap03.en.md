*(Hospital room. Blinds drawn. The father is lying down, the sheets pulled up to his chest. His hands lie flat on the sheet, too still. He is looking at the ceiling. The two sons are standing on either side of the bed. No one has sat down.)*

**Tazar:**
You sold the book.

*(The youngest does not respond. He looks at his father’s hands.)*

**Tazar:**
I got the call from the editor this morning. In the waiting room. While we waited to find out if he would walk again.

**2D:**
I needed money.

**Tazar:** *(a laugh with nothing in it)*
Of course.

*(He takes two steps, stops in front of the window, his back to them.)*

**2D:**
Say something true for once. Not just “of course”.

**Tazar:**
What do you want me to say.

**2D:**
I don't know. Anything. But not that.

*(The father blinks. Nothing else.)*

**Tazar:** *(still facing the window)*
Do you remember the last time we two really had a fight? Really.

**2D:**
No

**Tazar:**
Me neither.

*(He finally turns around.)*

**Tazar:**
We never yelled. We just... everyone did our thing, in our corner, in silence, for twenty years. And look where that got us.

**2D:**
It has nothing to do with silence. It was a beam that gave way.

**Tazar:**
A beam that I touched, yes. Go ahead and say it.

**2D:**
I didn't say it.

**Tazar:**
You thought it.

**2D:**
I thought a lot of things. Like: why didn't you wait for the craftsman. Like: why can you ever just wait.

*(The eldest closes his eyes for a second, takes it.)*

**Tazar:**
Go ahead, keep going. It's time, right? Dad can't move, he can just listen. Now is the time or never for you to empty your bag.

**2D:**
Stop.

**Tazar:**
No, seriously. I've never seen you empty your bag. Never. So go ahead.

*(A different silence this time. The youngest looks at his brother, then at the window, then back at his brother.)*

**2D:**
I'm afraid you'll be mad at me for the rest of your life for something you did yourself.

*(Nothing. The eldest does not respond immediately. He approaches the bed, very slowly.)*

**Tazar:**
I'm afraid the same. Except that for me it's for something that I really did.

*(He stops in front of the bed. Looks at his father. His voice changes, lower.)*

**Tazar:**
Dad. *(Nothing.)*
I know you hear.
*(He takes a breath, searches for words, can't find any suitable ones, goes ahead anyway.)*
The house. I'll take care of it. I don't know how yet, but I'll take care of it.

*(He places his hand on his father's, for a moment, awkwardly, as if he didn't know if the gesture was allowed.)*

**Tazar:**
I'm sorry.

*(These are the only simple words he has said since the beginning. Neither he nor the younger one moves for a moment. Then the older one withdraws his hand, stands up, suddenly regains his usual, harsher voice.)*

**Tazar:** *(to his brother, without looking at him)*
You can go back and write, if you want. There's not much more to observe here.

*(He leaves. The door closes with a dull noise. The younger son remains standing for a long moment. He looks at his father. The father looks at him in return. Neither of them speaks. The youngest ends up sitting down, finally, on the chair that is too low. His hands clasped between his knees.)*

**2J:** *(very low, almost to himself)*
I don't know what to tell you either, dad.

*(The father turns his head slightly towards him. Nothing else. Outside, in the corridor, footsteps moving away, then nothing.)*
